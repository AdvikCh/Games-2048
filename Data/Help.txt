Guide for 2048
================================================================================

* Controls:
  * Setting:
    * 'esc' closes the game;
    * '-' & '=' toggle COLOUR_DARKness;
    * '/' or 'h' opens this [guide];
    * 'left' or 'right' change the board's width;
  * Playing:
    * 'enter' or 'space' start the game;
    * 'w' or 'up' shift the blocks up;
    * 'a' or 'left' shift the blocks left;
    * 's' or 'down' shift the blocks down;
    * 'd' or 'right' shift the blocks right;
    * 'r' clears the board & score;
